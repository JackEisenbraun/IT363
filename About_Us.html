WINKS TOWING SERVICE

FAMILY-OWNED, CENTRAL ILLINOIS

Winks Towing Service, established in 1970, is a locally operated, family-owned business in Bloomington Illinois.
We continue to care about our customers and strive to offer you the best possible service at an affordable rate.

We are a full-service gas station, store, and towing business.

Only a short drive from I-55/74 at the Market Street exit, our team works to get you back on the road in no time.

ABOUT US

THE WINKS FAMILY

Charles Winks started the towing, full-service gas station, and automotive shop in 1970.

After retiring in 2022, Charles passed the business to his children, John and Lisa. They keep the family business going and work to continue to provide affordable, personalized customer service.

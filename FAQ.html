FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you service to?

We service all around Central Illinois, including Bloomington, Normal, Peoria, Springfield, Clinton, Hudson, and more!

My vehicle has been in an accident, what now?

If you have full coverage insurance, contact your insurance company agent and tell them where your vehicle is located. They will usually handle it from then on.

Do you sell parts?

No, we do not sell parts. There are many places in town that will be able to get you the parts you need.

Do you accept credit cards?

Yes! We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and Discover cards.

Do you accept personal checks?

No, we do not.

What are the requirements for entry to the impound yard?

Only the following are allowed to enter:

  • Registered owner of the vehicle or their insurance agent
  • Insurance Adjusters
  • Police Officers

Other rules:

  • No one under the age of 16 will be allowed to enter
  • Make sure to wear close-toed shoes

My vehicle is broken, can I work on it in your lot?

No, you will need to make arrangements to have the vehicle removed from the lot and taken to a repair shop.

My car is impounded in your lot, but I want my personal belongings from it. What should I do?

As per Illinois statute 625 ILCS 5/4-203, only the following items are permitted to be released from impounded vehicles:

  • Food
  • Medicine
  • Perishable Property
  • Driver's License
  • Cash, credit cards, or checks
  • Wallets / Purses

All other content of the vehicle will be held until the bill against the vehicle is settled. This includes tools, clothing, radios, speakers, books, etc.

RECOVERY WORK

Here at Wink's Towing, we offer professional vehicle recovery services for a wide variety of situations. Whatever your situation may be, our team is more than capable of handling whatever life throws at you.

Below are a few examples of recovery services that we offer.

Vehicle Recovery

We provide traditional recovery services for vehicles that have been involved in accidents or are unable to continue driving safely.

Heavy Vehicles

Our towing equipment allows us to assist with larger vehicles, as well as more difficult recovery situations.

Off-Road Recovery

We are capable of recovering vehicles that may have exited the roadway or become stuck in difficult to access locations.

Additional Recovery Information

Each recovery situation is different. Please contact us for more information about our different recovery services.
